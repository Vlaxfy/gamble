How to play

• Move with WASD or the arrow keys, jump with Space, drag the screen to look around, scroll to zoom. On phones use the joystick and the jump button.
• Everyone plays the same live round. If a race is already running you spectate until the next one.
• Fall in the water and you go back to the start.
• Press 1 (or tap the hand) to push. You have to actually touch someone, and they fly!
• Finish to earn coins: 1st place pays 3x, 2nd 2x, 3rd 1.5x. Harder maps pay more.
• Press Enter to chat. Be nice: bad words get you timed out.
